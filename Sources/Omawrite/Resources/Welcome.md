# A little space to think

Welcome to **Omawrite**. A quiet place for your words, made for the Mac.

There’s no workspace to organize, no account to create, and nothing between you and the page. Just open a file, or start writing.

## Make yourself at home

Your words live in ordinary Markdown files. Use **bold** for emphasis, *italics* for a softer voice, and `code` when you need to be precise. Inline formatting steps out of the way as you move to the next paragraph.

> A sentence, then another. That’s how a page begins.

## A few small things

- Press Command B for bold, Command I for italic.
- Paste a URL over selected words to make a link.
- Press Return to continue a list, and Return again to leave it.
- Find anything with Command F. Replace with Option Command F.
- Try Focus Mode with Shift Command D.

The word count is in the corner. Your documents use the Mac’s autosave and recovery system. Light and dark follow your system, or you can choose a theme in Settings.

## Yours to keep

Save with Command S. Open your files in any editor, keep them in any folder, and make this little space your own.
